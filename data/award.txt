GENERAL RETAIL INDUSTRY (DEMO) AWARD

CLAUSE 14 — CLASSIFICATIONS

14.1 Retail Employee Level 1
An employee who has not yet completed 6 months' retail experience and performs basic
duties such as stacking shelves, price marking and cleaning, under direct supervision.

14.2 Retail Employee Level 2
An employee who has completed the equivalent of 6 months' retail experience or induction
training, and who performs sales duties, operates a cash register, or provides customer
service without direct, constant supervision.

14.3 Retail Employee Level 3
An employee who has completed the equivalent of 12 months' retail experience, exercises
some responsibility for stock control, cash reconciliation, or the supervision of one or
more Level 1 or Level 2 employees on a shift.

14.4 Retail Employee Level 4
An employee who supervises the work of other employees, has full responsibility for a
section or department, or holds a trade qualification relevant to their duties.

CLAUSE 18 — MINIMUM WEEKLY RATES

18.1 The minimum weekly rate for a full-time adult employee, based on a 38-hour week, is:
  (a) Retail Employee Level 1 — $915.40 per week
  (b) Retail Employee Level 2 — $945.20 per week
  (c) Retail Employee Level 3 — $985.70 per week
  (d) Retail Employee Level 4 — $1,032.10 per week

18.2 The minimum hourly rate is calculated by dividing the applicable weekly rate by 38.

CLAUSE 22 — CASUAL EMPLOYMENT

22.1 A casual employee is engaged and paid as such.

22.2 A casual employee must be paid the minimum hourly rate applicable to their
classification under clause 18, plus a casual loading of 25%.

22.3 The casual loading is paid instead of entitlements to annual leave, personal leave,
and other benefits provided to full-time and part-time employees under this award.

22.4 A casual employee must be paid for a minimum of 3 hours' work on each occasion they
are required to attend work.

CLAUSE 27 — ORDINARY HOURS AND PENALTY RATES

27.1 Ordinary hours of work for a full-time employee are an average of 38 hours per week,
worked between 7:00 am and 9:00 pm, Monday to Friday, and 7:00 am and 6:00 pm on Saturday.

27.2 Saturday penalty rate: an employee who works ordinary hours on a Saturday must be paid
125% of the minimum hourly rate.

27.3 Sunday penalty rate: an employee who works ordinary hours on a Sunday must be paid
150% of the minimum hourly rate for a full-time or part-time employee, and 175% of the
minimum hourly rate for a casual employee (inclusive of casual loading).

27.4 Public holiday penalty rate: an employee who works on a public holiday must be paid
225% of the minimum hourly rate for a full-time or part-time employee, and 250% of the
minimum hourly rate for a casual employee (inclusive of casual loading).

CLAUSE 31 — OVERTIME

31.1 A full-time employee who works in excess of 38 ordinary hours in a week, or outside
the span of hours in clause 27.1, is entitled to overtime rates.

31.2 Overtime rates are 150% of the minimum hourly rate for the first 3 hours, and 200%
of the minimum hourly rate after that, on any day.

31.3 A casual employee who works overtime is entitled to the overtime rate in 31.2
calculated on their ordinary hourly rate (i.e. excluding the casual loading, which is
not payable on overtime hours).

CLAUSE 35 — ALLOWANCES

35.1 Meal allowance: an employee required to work more than 1 hour of overtime without
being notified the previous day must be paid a meal allowance of $16.25, or be provided
with a meal.

35.2 First aid allowance: an employee designated by their employer as a workplace first
aid officer must be paid an additional $18.50 per week.

35.3 Uniform allowance: where an employer requires an employee to wear a uniform that is
not supplied free of charge, the employer must pay an allowance of $6.35 per week.

CLAUSE 40 — BREAKS

40.1 An employee who works more than 5 hours on a shift is entitled to an unpaid meal
break of at least 30 minutes.

40.2 An employee who works a shift of 4 hours or more is entitled to one paid 10-minute
rest break.
